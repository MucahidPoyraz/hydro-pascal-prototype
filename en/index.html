From Türkiye To The World

Expand your horizons by joining our global network; partner with us for uncompromising quality, sustainable business success, and strategic pricing.

HydroPascal was established in 2019 with the mission of serving the agricultural machinery spare parts industry. The company specializes in the production of high-quality spare parts for a wide range of both classic and modern tractors, including brands such as Massey Ferguson, Ford, New Holland, John Deere, Case, Renault, Zetor, Landini, Fendt, Deutz, Same.

In addition to its manufacturing capabilities, HydroPascal has developed strategic partnerships that enable it to provide casting and forging supply solutions under the PascalForge and PascalCast brands. Through this integrated approach, the company delivers reliable, durable, and performance-driven components to meet the evolving demands of the agricultural sector.
