Casting Excellence

PascalCast

Precision in Every Pour

PascalCast represents the highest standards in casting technology, with advanced production infrastructure and engineering expertise in gray iron casting, steel casting, ductile (nodular) iron casting, and aluminum casting. Through the use of modern casting techniques, precise process control systems, and high-quality raw materials, every stage of production is optimized to ensure maximum durability, structural integrity, and superior performance.

Equipped with high-precision molding technologies, automation-supported production lines, and rigorous quality control procedures, PascalCast is capable of manufacturing even the most complex and high-strength components with exceptional accuracy. Driven by a commitment to continuous improvement and innovation, the company delivers cutting-edge casting solutions, providing reliable, sustainable, and high-performance products to its customers.

Ductile and Gray Iron Castings Products

PascalCast is a leading manufacturer of ductile and gray iron castings, offering a comprehensive portfolio of cast iron products designed to meet the diverse requirements of multiple industry sectors. With a strong focus on quality and performance, the company delivers durable and high-precision castings for a wide range of applications, including automotive, construction, agriculture, and power generation.

Steel Casting

PascalCast is a dedicated specialist in steel casting, delivering high-strength, precision-engineered components designed for the most demanding industrial applications. With a strong focus on metallurgical excellence and strict process control, the company produces steel castings that provide outstanding toughness, wear resistance, and structural reliability across key sectors such as heavy machinery, construction, automotive, energy, and industrial systems.

Our steel casting production is based on advanced alloy engineering, carefully controlled melting and pouring processes, and highly regulated manufacturing standards. Each component is produced under strict quality parameters to ensure superior mechanical performance, dimensional accuracy, and long-term durability even in extreme operating conditions.

Through continuous innovation, rigorous testing, and a quality-driven production philosophy, PascalCast ensures that every steel casting meets international standards and delivers consistent, reliable performance where strength and resilience are essential.

HydroPascal was established in 2019 with the mission of serving the agricultural machinery spare parts industry. The company specializes in the production of high-quality spare parts for a wide range of both classic and modern tractors, including brands such as Massey Ferguson, Ford, New Holland, John Deere, Case, Renault, Zetor, Landini, Fendt, Deutz, Same.

In addition to its manufacturing capabilities, HydroPascal has developed strategic partnerships that enable it to provide casting and forging supply solutions under the PascalForge and PascalCast brands. Through this integrated approach, the company delivers reliable, durable, and performance-driven components to meet the evolving demands of the agricultural sector.
